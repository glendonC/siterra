## Inspection notes for MKT-09 onward

- **Extracted (targeted):** the page was fetched and an automated extraction step answered targeted questions about it. This is not a full manual read. Where a row says verbatim confirmation was obtained, the load-bearing sentences were returned word for word and checked.
- **Full page text read in a browser:** the rendered page text was read in full.
- **Access this pass:** lens.tech, help.openai.com and the Esri article returned HTTP 403 to the fetch tool and were read in a browser instead. The Edison Scientific announcement URL returned 404, so MKT-19 relies on the preprint abstract and the company homepage. The public Planetary Computer homepage rendered no text. No product in MKT-09 to MKT-20 was signed into, run, purchased or tested, and nobody was contacted.
- **Matrix re-reads:** MKT-03, MKT-05 and MKT-06 were re-extracted and MKT-04 was re-read in a browser on 26 October 2026 to support the capability matrix in [landscape.md](landscape.md). Observed on those re-reads: the MKT-05 page does not describe citations or step visibility; MKT-06 now also describes plan approval before an agent starts, review before release, background and scheduled agents, cross-session memory and logging of every step; MKT-04 states that a few ArcGIS assistants are generally available while many are beta or preview, that Maps SDK AI components and the Data Explorer template are beta, and that MCP support is being worked toward.
- **Volatility:** MKT-10 changes monthly and MKT-15 retired a named product within the past week. Recheck both before any public comparison.
