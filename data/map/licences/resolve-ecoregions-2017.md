# RESOLVE Ecoregions 2017: licence and credit for the rangeland biomes layer

Source: RESOLVE Ecoregions 2017, https://storage.googleapis.com/teow2016/Ecoregions2017.zip (149,248,653 bytes, SHA-256 `be36d6209e443038d02e309f0447c6e7f2a62f5fe60c605ffe90d064952f2a60`). Every record's LICENSE field reads "CC-BY 4.0" (Creative Commons Attribution 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Credit wherever the layer is shown: "Ecoregions 2017 (c) Resolve, CC BY 4.0".

Citation: Dinerstein, E. et al. (2017). An Ecoregion-Based Approach to Protecting Half the Terrestrial Realm. BioScience 67(6): 534-545. https://doi.org/10.1093/biosci/bix014.

Method: the Rangelands Atlas (ILRI and partners, 2021, CC BY 4.0), as used by the UNCCD Global Land Outlook thematic report on rangelands and pastoralists (Herrera 2024): rangelands are seven of the fourteen WWF terrestrial biomes. The layer is derived and changed from the source (rasterized and averaged to 0.25 degree).
