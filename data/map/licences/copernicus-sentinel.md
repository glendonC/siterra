# Copernicus Sentinel data: notice for the summer true-colour drape

The drape is made from Copernicus Sentinel-2 Level-2A data (Earth Search legacy `sentinel-2-l2a`, five dates from 26 July to 30 August 2025), composited and stretched for display. Copernicus Sentinel data are free to use under the European Commission's legal notice on the use of Copernicus Sentinel data and service information: https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/licences/ec-sentinel.

The drape is modified Sentinel data. Credit wherever it is shown:

"Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2025"

It is a display image: no value is read from it, and its colours are a fixed display stretch, not calibrated reflectance.
